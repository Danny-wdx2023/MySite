HiTechThoughts

2025-08-23

When ChatGPT became popular all over the world, everyone was amazed by LLM; When you hold an Apple Vision Pro, you get the smart ideas behind spatial computing; When self-driving cars run on city roads, we’re surprised by how much AI has improved. Technology is moving very fast, changing how we live, work and think.

In this fast-moving world of new ideas, I feel both excited and a little confused. New technologies have a lot of possibilities, but they’re often hard to understand - with difficult words and new things coming out all the time. Even people who like tech can find it hard. That’s why I started this blog.

First, I want to make new tech easier to understand. Ideas like machine learning or blockchain use a lot of hard words, which can scare people who aren’t in the field. My posts will break these ideas into simple parts, explaining how they work and how they’re used in real life. Whether you’re a student, someone who works in a job, or just someone who’s interested, I want tech to feel easy to get into.

Second, it’s a place to share useful things I’ve learned. When I try out new tech, I run into problems, make mistakes, and find ways to fix them. Writing about these experiences can help others—like talking about the tricky parts of new computer programs or how new trends might affect different jobs. These real stories add to what you can learn from books, helping readers use tech better.

Third, I hope to build a group of people who are interested. Tech gets better when people work together and share ideas. I want readers to leave comments, ask questions, and even disagree. These talks can give new ideas, make us think again, and help us understand things better—turning the blog into a place where we all learn together.

Finally, the blog helps me keep learning. When I research and write, I have to look deeper into each topic. It shows me what I don’t know and makes me want to find out more. Writing for the blog is a way to teach myself, and sharing it with others makes me want to keep exploring.

In a world where tech changes faster than we can keep up, this blog is a bridge between new ideas and understanding. It makes hard things simple, shares what I’ve learned, and helps us all grow. I hope it lets more people get involved with tech—using it and shaping it to make things better.
